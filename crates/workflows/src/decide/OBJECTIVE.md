# Objective

Take the decisions development used to wait on a person for, and tell the
person what was decided.

**Trigger.** A task, a milestone or a rules' issue needs a decision: an
ambiguity a session stopped on, a review its repairs did not clear, a
question on the plan, a request no agent could read.

**What it does.** Reads what is asked and everything said about it, decides
in one short session — answer the question, merge as it is, repair once
more, or cut the task — writes the decision where the next session reads
it, carries it out, and tells the person, saying how to overrule it.

**Why it matters.** A plant that waits on a person for every doubt runs at
the person's pace. Most doubts are settled by the design already decided;
the person reads the few decisions after the fact, and the work goes on.

**What it deliberately does not do.** It never decides over an answer the
person already wrote. It never spends money, creates an account or holds a
credential: what only a person can do stays a task for the person. It never
pays a second session to disagree with the first: one label, one decision.

**What "done" looks like.** The decision is under `## Decision`, carried out,
labelled, and told — or the person had already answered, and nothing was paid.
